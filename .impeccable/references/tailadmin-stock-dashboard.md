## Binding interpretation

- These three images are the standing visual benchmark for new Valuation Engine surfaces. Future direction rounds start from them rather than treating them as temporary inspiration.
- TailAdmin V2 is the lead reference. TailAdmin V1 and NextAdmin are supporting references when they solve clarity, density, or responsive composition better.
- Use the references for information architecture, hierarchy, spatial rhythm, control density, and analytical composition—not for copying their branding, content, or proprietary assets.
- The user's later instructions override the source images where they conflict: the product stays black/graphite rather than white, uses sharp 4–5px structural corners, removes the login/overview opening detour, and uses an honest traditional line-chart treatment for annual Revenue and Free Cash Flow rather than fabricating stock-price or OHLC history.
- The approved implementation remains the source of truth for product behavior, data semantics, accessibility, and the public-model/private-data boundary.
