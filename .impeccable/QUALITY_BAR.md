# Quality bar — institutional graphite research console

This is a code-led build calibrated against the user-pinned TailAdmin V1, TailAdmin V2, and NextAdmin information architecture, with the user's later explicit dark-theme and traditional-chart direction taking precedence over the earlier light decision references.

- **Frame:** a near-black 220px navigation rail, a 64px graphite utility bar, and an edge-to-edge `#080b10` working field. The shell remains legible and operational at 320px through a dark bottom navigation bar.
- **Depth:** blue-black panels use fine structural borders and 4–5px corners; resting shadows are absent. No glass, neon, decorative gradients, fake bevels, or floating-card stacks.
- **Typography:** Instrument Sans/Outfit establish compact display hierarchy; IBM Plex Sans carries body copy; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for audit-oriented data labels where already present. Tight tracking does not exceed `-0.04em`.
- **Controls:** restrained periwinkle is the sole interaction accent. Every mobile primary control and tab meets a 44px touch target. Focus remains visible.
- **Data:** model and market values use tabular figures. Green/red mean real outcome polarity only; Bear/Bull remain neutral scenarios.
- **Density:** first viewports show the operator's next action, latest state, and cross-check context without fabricated values or ornamental widgets.
- **Signature device:** the populated workspace holds a dominant, traditional dual-line annual forecast beside a sticky Thesis Rail whose scenario state, inputs, provenance, and market comparison remain in view.
- **Chart:** revenue and free cash flow use distinct thin lines, dual labeled axes, a quiet grid, exact legend values, and a hover crosshair. No area wash, bars, fabricated candlesticks, or line-drawing animation.
- **Motion:** short control-state transitions only; financial charts render immediately. Reduced-motion preferences disable result entrance and loading animations.
