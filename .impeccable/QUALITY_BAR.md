# Quality bar — disciplined research console

This is a code-led build calibrated against the user-pinned TailAdmin V1, TailAdmin V2, and NextAdmin family and the generated decision critique references.

- **Frame:** a white 220px navigation rail, a slim white utility bar, and a cool `#f6f8fc` working field. The shell remains legible and operational at 320px through a bottom navigation bar.
- **Depth:** white panels use fine structural borders; shadows are limited to controls and brand marks where they communicate lift. No glass, neon, decorative gradients, or floating-card stacks.
- **Typography:** Instrument Sans/Outfit establish compact display hierarchy; IBM Plex Sans carries body copy; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for audit-oriented data labels where already present. Tight tracking does not exceed `-0.04em`.
- **Controls:** cobalt is the sole interaction accent. Every mobile primary control and tab meets a 44px touch target. Focus remains visible.
- **Data:** model and market values use tabular figures. Green/red mean real outcome polarity only; Bear/Bull remain neutral scenarios.
- **Density:** first viewports show the operator's next action, latest state, and cross-check context without fabricated values or ornamental widgets.
- **Signature device:** the populated workspace holds a dominant forecast beside a sticky Thesis Rail whose scenario state, inputs, provenance, and market comparison remain in view.
- **Motion:** short state transitions only; reduced-motion preferences disable the result entrance and loading animations.
